Let  r = radius,  h = height
of the cylinder.
Sphere constraint:
r² + (h/2)² = R²
V = πr²h  →  sub r² = R² − h²/4
V = π(R² − h²/4)h = πR²h − πh³/4
dV/dh = πR² − 3πh²/4 = 0
⇒  h² = 4R²/3,  h = 2R/√3
∴  r² = R² − R²/3 = 2R²/3
r = R√(2/3)  ❖
